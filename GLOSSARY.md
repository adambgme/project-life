# Glossary

- **Life grid:** the boxes of a whole life: one row per year of age, in Years, Months or Weeks.
- **Box:** one unit of the grid. In the Weeks view a row has 52 boxes starting on a birthday; the 52nd box holds the
  last 8 or 9 days before the next birthday.
- **Lived box:** a box entirely before the current one. **This week** is the box containing today.
- **Birthday (29 February):** in common years, someone born on 29 February has their birthday on 1 March (UK legal
  convention).
- **Profile:** the person's details kept in their browser: birth date (and later sex, nation, grid length).
- **Era / Moment / Goal:** a date range / a single past date / a future target on the grid (T5, T6).
